# Office Lurgy

A daily deduction puzzle: a bug is doing the rounds of an open-plan office, and the player works out who has caught it using only what the coworkers already found have said.

## Language

**Coworker**:
One person on the board: a member of the cast who is in the office that week, sitting at one desk, and either sick or well.
_Avoid_: suspect, colleague, employee, person

**Cast**:
Everyone who works at the office, whether or not they are in that week. Each member has a unique first name and a department, and keeps their personality from week to week.
_Avoid_: characters, staff

**Week**:
The seven puzzles from Monday to Sunday that share the same coworkers at the same desks, and at most one story, which ends on Friday. Saturday and Sunday are Overtime.
_Avoid_: episode, season

**Overtime**:
The weekend puzzles, Saturday and Sunday, where one sick coworker is the fibber.
_Avoid_: weekend mode, bonus round

**Story**:
The tale a week's flavour tells, from Monday's setup to Friday's finale. The story never says or suggests whether a coworker on the board is sick or well.
_Avoid_: plot, arc, lore

**Quiet week**:
A Week with no story: the build picks who's in from the cast, and coworkers say only their general lines.
_Avoid_: filler week, empty week

**Sick**:
Has caught the bug. Every coworker on the board is either sick or well, never both, and never unknown to the puzzle, only to the player.
_Avoid_: infected, contagious, criminal

**Well**:
Has not caught the bug. The opposite of sick.
_Avoid_: healthy, clean, innocent

**Office manager**:
The player's role: the one working out who is sick so they can be sent home.
_Avoid_: detective, HR

**Desk**:
One position on the office floor. Every coworker sits at exactly one desk, and the desks form the grid of the board.
_Avoid_: cell, seat, tile

**Department**:
The group a coworker works in (Sales, IT, HR, Finance…). Clues can refer to everyone in a department. Each has a lanyard colour, worn in its coworkers' portraits, and a short code (FIN, LGL…); a tile shows both.
_Avoid_: profession, team, role

**Neighbours**:
The coworkers at the desks touching a coworker's desk, diagonals included: up to eight.
_Avoid_: adjacent, nearby

**Clue**:
A statement a revealed coworker makes about who is sick or well: one coworker, or how many in one or more groups. Every clue is true, whoever says it, except the fibber's on Overtime, and tells the office manager something the clues and declarations before it haven't already. A clue names coworkers in the first person when they are the speaker ("my neighbours", "I").
_Avoid_: hint, statement, evidence

**Fibber**:
On Overtime, the one sick coworker whose clue is false. The office manager knows there is exactly one, and that they are sick, but not who.
_Avoid_: liar, cheat

**Flavour**:
What a revealed coworker says that is not a clue: a joke, a general line or a line of the week's story in place of a clue, or a short joke after one. A coworker with nothing new to tell says a joke or general line in place of a clue. Flavour never carries or suggests logic.
_Avoid_: banter, filler

**Group**:
A set of desks a clue talks about: a row, a column, a coworker's neighbours, the desks between two coworkers, the corners, the edges, or a department.
_Avoid_: unit, area, zone, region

**Between**:
The desks in a straight line strictly between two coworkers in the same row or column, not counting the two themselves. When the line runs to the edge it reads as above, below, to the left of or to the right of a coworker.
_Avoid_: from-to, inside

**Above / below / left / right**:
Anywhere in the same column (above, below) or the same row (left, right), seen from the office manager's side of the board, not just the next desk.
_Avoid_: over, under

**Directly**:
Only the next desk in that direction: "directly below" is the one desk underneath.
_Avoid_: right next to, immediately

**Connected**:
Forming one unbroken chain of desks touching side to side, never diagonally. A single coworker counts as connected.
_Avoid_: adjacent, together, grouped

**Most**:
More than every other row, column or department, never tied: "Column D has the most sick people" is false if column A has as many.
_Avoid_: biggest, top

**One of the**:
A coworker and at least one other: "Kev is one of the sick people in row 2" means Kev is sick and so is someone else in row 2.
_Avoid_: among

**Declare**:
The office manager's move: naming a coworker as sick or well, or on Overtime calling BS on one. A declaration is accepted only when it is both correct and forced.
_Avoid_: guess, mark, flag, accuse

**Call BS**:
On Overtime, declaring a revealed sick coworker the fibber. Forced once their clue is false on every board that fits everything else known. An Overtime puzzle is solved when every coworker is declared and BS is called on the fibber.
_Avoid_: accuse, expose, flag

**Forced**:
A coworker whose state is the same in every board that fits everything known so far: every revealed clue and every coworker already declared. On Overtime a board fits when every revealed clue is true except at most one sick coworker's, the fibber's.
_Avoid_: provable, certain, deducible, obvious

**Hint**:
Help the office manager asks for: first the fewest clues that force some coworker, then, if asked again, that coworker too. The hint stays on that coworker either way.
_Avoid_: tip, clue

**Difficulty**:
How hard a puzzle is: Easy, Medium or Hard, set by its hardest step, the most clues the office manager must combine to find any forced coworker.
_Avoid_: level, rating

**Mask**:
What a sick or well declaration rejected with "Not enough evidence" leaves on a coworker: they answer back with a remark and put on a face mask. A wrong declaration and one not yet forced get the same mask and the same kind of remark, so neither reveals which it was. A coworker wears at most one.
_Avoid_: mistake, error, strike, wrong guess

**Complaint**:
What calling BS rejected with "Not enough evidence" leaves on a coworker: they answer back indignantly and make a complaint. Like a mask, a wrong call and one not yet forced look the same. A coworker makes at most one.
_Avoid_: mask, strike, grievance

**Remark**:
What a coworker says back when they get a mask. Like flavour, a remark never carries or suggests logic.
_Avoid_: comeback, retort, taunt

**Taunt**:
The game's own jab at the office manager, after a hint or a solve. A masked coworker's remark is theirs, not a taunt; otherwise coworkers make clues and flavour.
_Avoid_: diss, insult, banter

**Verdict**:
The taunt on solving a puzzle, chosen by how many coworkers wear a mask, make a complaint or carry a hint: none, a few, or many.
_Avoid_: score, grade, rating

**Result**:
What a solved puzzle leaves to share, including the time since it was first opened, the verdict, and a grid showing which coworkers wear a mask, make a complaint or carry a hint, and on Overtime where the fibber sat.
_Avoid_: score, scorecard
