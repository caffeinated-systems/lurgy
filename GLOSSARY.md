# Office bug

A daily deduction puzzle: a bug is doing the rounds of an open-plan office, and the player works out who has caught it using only what the coworkers already found have said.

## Language

**Coworker**:
One person on the board. Each coworker has a name, a department and a desk, and is either sick or well.
_Avoid_: suspect, colleague, employee, person

**Sick**:
Has caught the bug. Every coworker on the board is either sick or well, never both, and never unknown to the puzzle, only to the player.
_Avoid_: infected, contagious, criminal

**Well**:
Has not caught the bug. The opposite of sick.
_Avoid_: healthy, clean, innocent

**Office manager**:
The player's role: the one working out who is sick so they can be sent home.
_Avoid_: detective, HR

**Desk**:
One position on the office floor. Every coworker sits at exactly one desk, and the desks form the grid of the board.
_Avoid_: cell, seat, tile

**Department**:
The group a coworker works in (Sales, IT, HR, Finance…). Clues can refer to everyone in a department.
_Avoid_: profession, team, role

**Neighbours**:
The coworkers at the desks touching a coworker's desk, diagonals included: up to eight.
_Avoid_: adjacent, nearby
