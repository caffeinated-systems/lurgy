# Office bug

A daily deduction puzzle: a bug is doing the rounds of an open-plan office, and the player works out who has caught it using only what the coworkers already found have said.

## Language

**Coworker**:
One person on the board. Each coworker has a name, a department and a desk, and is either sick or well. A coworker is a member of the cast who is in the office this week.
_Avoid_: suspect, colleague, employee, person

**Cast**:
Everyone who works at the office, whether or not they are in this week. Each member has a unique first name and a department, and keeps their personality from week to week.
_Avoid_: characters, staff

**Week**:
The five puzzles from Monday to Friday that share the same twenty coworkers at the same desks, and one story.
_Avoid_: episode, season

**Story**:
A week's flavour lines, written to run from Monday's setup to Friday's finale. The story never says or hints whether a coworker on the board is sick or well.
_Avoid_: plot, arc, lore

**Sick**:
Has caught the bug. Every coworker on the board is either sick or well, never both, and never unknown to the puzzle, only to the player.
_Avoid_: infected, contagious, criminal

**Well**:
Has not caught the bug. The opposite of sick.
_Avoid_: healthy, clean, innocent

**Office manager**:
The player's role: the one working out who is sick so they can be sent home.
_Avoid_: detective, HR

**Desk**:
One position on the office floor. Every coworker sits at exactly one desk, and the desks form the grid of the board.
_Avoid_: cell, seat, tile

**Department**:
The group a coworker works in (Sales, IT, HR, Finance…). Clues can refer to everyone in a department.
_Avoid_: profession, team, role

**Neighbours**:
The coworkers at the desks touching a coworker's desk, diagonals included: up to eight.
_Avoid_: adjacent, nearby

**Clue**:
A true statement a revealed coworker makes about how many coworkers are sick or well in one or more groups. Every clue is true, whoever says it. A clue names coworkers in the first person when they are the speaker ("my neighbours", "I").
_Avoid_: hint, statement, evidence

**Flavour**:
What a revealed coworker says instead of a clue: a joke or a line of the week's story. Flavour never carries logic, nor hints at it.
_Avoid_: banter, filler

**Group**:
A set of desks a clue talks about: a row, a column, a coworker's neighbours, the desks between two coworkers, the corners, the edges, or a department.
_Avoid_: unit, area, zone, region

**Between**:
The desks in a straight line strictly between two coworkers in the same row or column, not counting the two themselves. When the line runs to the edge it reads as above, below, to the left of or to the right of a coworker.
_Avoid_: from-to, inside

**Above / below / left / right**:
Anywhere in the same column (above, below) or the same row (left, right), seen from the office manager's side of the board, not just the next desk.
_Avoid_: over, under

**Directly**:
Only the next desk in that direction: "directly below" is the one desk underneath.
_Avoid_: right next to, immediately

**Connected**:
Forming one unbroken chain of desks touching side to side, never diagonally. A single coworker counts as connected.
_Avoid_: adjacent, together, grouped

**Declare**:
The office manager's move: naming a coworker as sick or well. A declaration is accepted only when it is both correct and forced.
_Avoid_: guess, mark, flag, accuse

**Forced**:
A coworker whose state is the same in every board that fits everything known so far: every revealed clue and every coworker already declared.
_Avoid_: provable, certain, deducible, obvious

**Hint**:
Help the office manager asks for: it points at one forced coworker and the fewest clues that force it.
_Avoid_: tip, clue

**Difficulty**:
How hard a puzzle is: Easy, Medium or Hard, set by its hardest step, the most clues the office manager must combine to find any forced coworker.
_Avoid_: level, rating
