## Monday
The auditors arrive without warning. Sophia is delighted. Kelly meets them at reception and wants to see some ID.

- Sophia: An audit, how lovely, I've baked them a tray of flapjacks and a feedback form.
- Kelly: The auditors can come in when they've signed the visitor book, and not a moment before.
- Quentin: I've told the auditors that every receipt is where it should be, more or less.

## Tuesday
Legal locks the stationery cupboard pending review. Procurement can't find the order book.

- Bernadette: The stationery cupboard is closed pending legal review, pens included, without prejudice.
- Ursula: I've filed the order book so thoroughly I'll need a purchase order to find it.
- Jas: Respectfully, the mandatory fun budget is in a drawer, and the drawer is giving cupboard.

## Wednesday
The auditors find a purchase order for a whole pallet of lanyards, and nobody knows where they are. Henry stays late to help with the figures, and so, for no reason anyone can see, does Gemma.

- Ffion: I've explained the escape room to the auditors, and they've asked me to stop.
- Mo: I've reconciled the petty cash, and I'm not going to say any more about it.
- Henry: I'm staying late for the audit, and that's all there is to it.

## Thursday
One of the auditors goes down with the lurgy, and Finance sends a card. The search for the lanyards goes on.

- Tania: The auditors asked for the IT password, so I've reported them for phishing.
- Steve: I've offered the auditors a tour of my shed, and they've said they'll think about it.
- Imogen: I've backed up the audit, and then backed up the backup, just in case.

## Friday
The pallet of lanyards turns up in the lift, where it has been all week. The audit is signed off. Fergus finds the lanyards and keeps one back.

- Barry: The lanyards were in the lift all along, chief, and the lift knew.
- Fergus: I've kept the nicest lanyard back, purely as a Facilities matter, no reason.
- Carol: To celebrate the audit, there will be a mandatory fun lunch, attendance noted.
