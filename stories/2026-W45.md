## Monday
Procurement spent Friday at an escape room on the company card, with Aiden from HR along to observe. None of them have come back to work. Quentin has seen the receipt.

- Quentin: Procurement put an escape room on the company card, and none of them came back.
- Barry: The escape room rang to say they'd found a lanyard and a lot of regret, chief.
- Carol: I've sent Procurement a card from all of us, which HR covers under the morale budget.

## Tuesday
The lurgy reaches reception. Cheryl is off, Kelly from Legal is holding the fort alone, and Rosa's absence means nobody knows anything. Fergus finds another reason to visit Marketing.

- Linda: With Rosa off, love, I've had to find out the gossip myself, and I'm not built for research.
- Fergus: I've put Marketing's chairs on my maintenance rota, purely professionally.
- Anika: Legally, the reception plant cannot sign for parcels, and I've told Kelly so.

## Wednesday
Kelly goes down too, and reception is empty. Bernadette responds with a memo. Gemma and Henry come back from lunch with the same sandwich and very different stories.

- Bernadette: I've issued a memo banning handshakes, high fives and anything Steve calls a bro hug.
- Kev: With reception empty, I've appointed myself head of lanyards and nobody can stop me.
- Gemma: My lunch plans are nobody's business, and I'd thank you not to make them anyone's.

## Thursday
Customer Services, all one of her, is off, and IT has lost Tania and Dan. The phones ring on, and anyone passing answers them.

- Ollie: I've been answering Customer Services' phones and I've apologised to my own mum.
- Erin: With Tania off, I've changed every password to something she would hate.
- Steve: I tried covering the phones, but a customer asked me to stop telling them about my shed.

## Friday
Ffion emails from home: she has talked the escape room into a full refund, and Aiden has finally solved the last clue. The door was never locked. Quentin is happier than anyone has ever seen him.

- Quentin: Ffion got a full refund on the escape room, so frankly she can stay home forever.
- Yusuf: I've read the escape room's waiver twice, and I'm sorry to say it's airtight.
- Priya: My chair has stopped squeaking, and I've decided it was never mine to worry about.
