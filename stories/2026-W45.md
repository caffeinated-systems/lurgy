## Monday
Procurement spent Friday at an escape room on the company card. None of them have come back to work. Quentin has seen the receipt.

- Quentin: Procurement put an escape room on the company card, and none of them came back.
- Barry: The escape room rang to say they'd found a lanyard and a lot of regret, chief.
- Carol: I've sent Procurement a card from all of us, which HR covers under the morale budget.

## Tuesday
The lurgy reaches reception. Cheryl is off, Vince is holding the fort alone, and Rosa's absence means nobody knows anything. Fergus finds another reason to visit Marketing.

- Linda: With Rosa off, love, I've had to find out the gossip myself and it's exhausting.

- Nigel: Legally, the reception plant cannot sign for parcels, and I've told Vince so.

## Wednesday
Vince goes down too, and reception is empty. Bernadette responds with a memo. Gemma and Hamid come back from lunch with the same sandwich and very different stories.

- Bernadette: I've issued a memo banning handshakes, high fives and anything Steve calls a bro hug.
- Kev: With reception empty, I've appointed myself head of lanyards and nobody can stop me.
- Gemma: Hamid and I happened to pick the same sandwich shop, which means nothing at all.

## Thursday
Customer Services is wiped out and IT has lost Tariq. The phones ring on, and anyone passing answers them.

- Ollie: I've been answering Customer Services' phones and I've apologised to my own mum.
- Erin: With Tariq off, I've changed every password to something he would hate.
- Steve: I tried covering the phones, but a customer asked me to stop telling them about my shed.

## Friday
Ffion emails from home: she has talked the escape room into a full refund, and Zach has finally solved the last clue. The door was never locked. Quentin is happier than anyone has ever seen him.

- Quentin: Ffion got a full refund on the escape room, so frankly she can stay home forever.
- Yusuf: Bernadette says the escape room's waiver is airtight, and I've never seen her so happy.
- Priya: Fergus has fixed my chair again, honestly, the man must never go home.
