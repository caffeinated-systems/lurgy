# The cast of Office Lurgy

The office is one big open-plan floor above a carpet warehouse on the ring road. Everyone hot-desks, and the booking system seats people alphabetically by first name, which nobody asked for and nobody can turn off. Ten departments share the floor and a kettle. The kitchen fridge is a crime scene, the lift has moods, and the reception plant is called Gary.

Notes for writers: about ten members carry the stories and the rest drop in. Keep the threads quiet. Nobody in the office knows about Henry and Gemma, and Priya has no idea about Fergus.

## Barry
Department: Facilities

A main character. Has been here since the building was fields, or says so. Calls everyone "chief", carries a stepladder he never seems to use, and has an anecdote for every ceiling tile. Fiercely loyal to the old boiler.

### Lines
- I've seen off more reorganisations than you've had hot dinners, chief.
- That ceiling tile has been like that since before you were born.
- Don't touch the boiler, it knows when you're nervous.
- I'm not saying it was better in my day, but the biscuits were.

### Jokes
- Take it from Barry.
- Seen it all, chief.
- Write that down.
