# The Bake Off

## In
Aiden, Anika, Barry, Carol, Dan, Erin, Fergus, Gemma, Henry, Imogen,
Kev, Linda, Milla, Mo, Nadia, Ollie, Priya, Sophia, Steve, Yusuf

## Away
- Cheryl: off with the lurgy, so the cake table has no one to guard it
- Ffion: off with the lurgy, her lemon drizzle entered by proxy
- Rosa: off with the lurgy, which means nobody knows who's baking what
- Quentin: off with the lurgy, so nobody is checking the bake-off's budget
- Bernadette: off with the lurgy, her memo on cake hygiene sent from bed
- Keisha: off with the lurgy, still on hold to the GP

## Monday
Carol launches the office charity bake-off: entries all week, judging on Friday, everything for a good cause. Barry has appointed himself judge. Cheryl was meant to run the cake table and is off.

- Carol: The charity bake-off is open, and I'd remind you that taking part is mandatory and fun.
- Barry: I've judged every bake-off since the building was fields, chief, and I'm not stopping now.
- Milla: I've rebranded the bake-off as a cake-led engagement moment, and it's huge, frankly.

## Tuesday
The first entries arrive. Ffion's lemon drizzle comes in a taxi without her. The cake table is unguarded.

- Linda: I've brought a coffee and walnut, love, and it has never lost a bake-off.
- Sophia: Before you take a slice, would you mind rating your experience of the cake table?
- Dan: I've entered a fruit cake, and the recipe is coming by letter, second class.

## Wednesday
Gemma and Henry turn up with the same Victoria sponge, on the same plate, by complete coincidence. Bernadette's memo on cake hygiene arrives from home.

- Gemma: My sponge is entirely my own recipe, and nobody helped, at all, so moving on.
- Anika: Technically a jaffa cake is a cake, and I've written to the judges about it.
- Yusuf: I've read the bake-off rules twice, and there is nothing in them about icing sugar.

## Thursday
The cake table fills up, and nobody is guarding it. Fergus enters a flapjack, for nobody in particular.

- Fergus: I've entered a flapjack, and I'd like it known it's for nobody in particular.
- Aiden: I may have registered the whole office for the bake-off, including the plant.
- Nadia: Mine's a carrot cake, and I'm not taking questions about the walnuts.

## Friday
Judging day. Barry tastes everything and can't decide. The charity does very nicely out of it.

- Barry: I've tasted every cake, chief, and the judging will continue next year.
- Priya: I've designed the certificates, each in its own font, and I regret nothing.
- Ollie: I've been asked not to call the bake-off a growth opportunity, so I won't.
