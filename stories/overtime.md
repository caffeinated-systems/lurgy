# Overtime

What everyone says at the weekend, when the office opens for double pay. Anyone on the board can say any of these on Saturday or Sunday, in place of their own lines and jokes.

Notes for writers: keep it about the overtime, the empty building, the money and where else they could be. Never about feeling ill, looking peaky or anyone's health, and never about who might be lying: a line must not hint at who's sick or who the fibber is.

## Lines
- Double time for answering emails nobody will read until Monday. Lovely.
- The heating's off at weekends, so I've brought a blanket and my principles.
- The office at the weekend: no meetings, no ice-breakers. Bliss.
- I've brought a packed lunch. The canteen's shut and I'm not paying for a taxi to the sandwich shop.
- The vending machine doesn't get restocked at weekends. Choose wisely.
- The whole floor to ourselves and we still all sit at our usual desks.
- The lift works better at weekends. Fewer people to sulk about.
- Gary the plant looks lonely at the weekend.
- I'm saving up for a holiday I'll be too busy to take.
- The car park's empty. I parked diagonally across four spaces, just because.
- The printer is just as broken at the weekend. Consistent, at least.
- Overtime sign-up sheet was full by Wednesday. Wednesday!
- I must really love money to give up my beach day for this.
- My mates are at the football. I'm at a spreadsheet.
- I was meant to be at my nan's birthday. She'd understand. She loves a bargain.
- Somewhere out there is a lazy morning with my name on it.
- The garden centre won't visit itself, but here we are.
- I've missed a wedding for this. Not mine, thankfully.
- I could be on the sofa watching a box set. Instead I'm watching the clock.
- The kids are at a theme park with their gran. I'm at the photocopier.
- The allotment's going to have something to say about this.
- I had brunch booked. Avocado's not cheap, hence the overtime.
